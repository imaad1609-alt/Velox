Exercise Details

CHEST • COMPOUND

Barbell Bench Press

DIFFICULTY: ADVANCED
EQUIPMENT

Barbell, Flat Bench

MUSCLE GROUP

Chest (Major/Minor)

Instructions

  1. 01.

    Position yourself on the flat bench with your eyes directly under the bar. Ensure your feet are planted firmly on the floor.

  2. 02.

    Grip the bar with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Wrap your thumbs around the bar (suicide grip is prohibited).

  3. 03.

    Retract your scapula and create a slight arch in your lower back. Unrack the bar and stabilize it over your mid-chest.

  4. 04.

    Lower the bar in a controlled eccentric phase to the nipple line. Touch lightly and press vertically to full lockout.
